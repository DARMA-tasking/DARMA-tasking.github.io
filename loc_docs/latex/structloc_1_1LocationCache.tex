struct loc::\+\+Location\+Cache$<$ Key\+T, Value\+T $>$}
LRU cache of resolved locations, keyed by entity id. 

On overflow past {\ttfamily max\+\_\+size} the least-\/recently-\/used entry is evicted. A {\ttfamily get} or re-\/{\ttfamily insert} promotes an entry to most-\/recently-\/used.
