\subsubsection*{template$<$typename KeyT, typename ValueT$>$\newline
struct loc::\+\+Loc\+Lookup$<$ Key\+T, Value\+T $>$}
Combined lookup over the home-\/node directory and the local LRU cache. 

On the home node ({\ttfamily this\+\_\+node} == home) locations are stored permanently in the directory; elsewhere they are cached and may be evicted.
