The internal structures are pure and single-\/rank testable. The coordinator adds the distributed protocol.\hypertarget{md__2loc_2docs_2md_2architecture_autotoc_md2}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{The resolver protocol}{The resolver protocol}}\label{md__2loc_2docs_2md_2architecture_autotoc_md2}
Every entity has a fixed {\bfseries{home}} rank (a deterministic function of its id, chosen by the embedder). The home holds the authoritative location in its {\ttfamily directory}; other ranks hold cached resolutions that may be evicted.

Control messages are point-\/to-\/point {\ttfamily Comm::\+send\texorpdfstring{$<$}{<}\&Coordinator::\+handler\texorpdfstring{$>$}{>}} calls:\+


\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item {\ttfamily update\+Home(id, node)} — a rank tells the home where an entity now lives.
\item {\ttfamily location\+Request(id, requester)} — a rank asks the home for a location.
\item {\ttfamily resolve\+Response(id, node)} — the home answers a request (and eagerly refreshes previously-\/answered ranks when the location later changes).
\item {\ttfamily exists\+Request} /\+ {\ttfamily exists\+Response} — the existence query pair.
\end{DoxyItemize}

Requests that reach the home before the entity is known are buffered ({\ttfamily pending\+\_\+home\+\_\+}) and answered on registration. Ranks that have been answered are remembered ({\ttfamily loc\+\_\+asks\+\_\+}) and {\bfseries{eagerly refreshed}} when the entity migrates, so caches converge without re-\/querying.\hypertarget{md__2loc_2docs_2md_2architecture_autotoc_md3}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{Instance registration is collective}{Instance registration is collective}}\label{md__2loc_2docs_2md_2architecture_autotoc_md3}
The coordinator registers itself with the communicator ({\ttfamily register\+Instance\+Collective}) in its constructor. Because the returned handle\textquotesingle{}s index must match across ranks, {\bfseries{every rank must construct its coordinator in the same order}}. This mirrors VT\textquotesingle{}s objgroup collective-\/creation requirement and is the embedder\textquotesingle{}s responsibility.\hypertarget{md__2loc_2docs_2md_2architecture_autotoc_md4}{}\doxysection{\texorpdfstring{What is deliberately out of scope}{What is deliberately out of scope}}\label{md__2loc_2docs_2md_2architecture_autotoc_md4}

\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Message forwarding /\+ routing of the entities\textquotesingle{} own messages (resolver-\/only).
\item The multi-\/instance {\ttfamily Location\+Manager}:\+ the embedder owns coordinator lifetime and instantiates one per logical entity space.
\item Any objgroup /\+ VT coupling:\+ the communication backend is injected through {\ttfamily loc::\+\+Communicator}, satisfied by standalone {\ttfamily comm::\+\+Comm\+MPI} or a VT adapter. 
\end{DoxyItemize}
